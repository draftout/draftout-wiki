Lobbies are unranked games for up to 4 players.
Create a Lobby from the main Draftout menu and share the code or enter a friend's code to join.

Lobby host can choose the board and settings, then select **Start** once everyone is ready.

## Boards and teams

| Board Setting | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Random | Generate a random board, without drafting. |
| Drafted | Two players take turns building a board. |
| Custom | Build the entire board yourself in the Board Builder. |

With **Single** teams, all players work together to complete the whole board. This mode is also known as **Blackout** (you can also play it alone).

With **Custom** teams, different teams race to complete the majority of the goals. Players with the same color share a team.
Lobby hosts with Supporter Tier 1 or above can assign teams to players (such as 2v2).

## Match settings

Lobby host can set:
- Board size, from 3×3 to 7×7.
- Time limit, from 10 to 180 minutes.
- Overtime, from 0 to 30 minutes.
- Countdown, from 5 to 120 seconds.
- A fixed world seed, random seed (or choose specific seed types).

Choosing which [seed types](/wiki/gameplay/seeds) can appear requires the host to have Supporter Tier 2 or above.

Before game starts, Lobby host can use the **Change Seed** button to generate a new world, or **Reroll Board** button to replace a random board.

## Creating Custom Boards

Choose **Custom** and open the Board Builder. Click on squares to select goals, and right click to edit goal data (e.g. color of wool or leather armor piece). You can save Custom Boards as files to reuse or share, or load a saved board to apply it to the Lobby.

Some goals are available in Board Builder even though they do not appear in regular games (goals which have been retired, or experimental goals). Use the [goal index](/wiki/goals) to read their completion requirements.

## Commands

The **Commands** setting can run up to 10 commands when players join the world or when the match starts. It can be used to give yourself items, teleport you to specific location (and whatever else you can think of).
