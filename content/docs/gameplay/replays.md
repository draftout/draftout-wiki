Replays let you watch a completed match inside Minecraft. You can switch between players, follow their movement and jump to goal completions.

## Download a replay

You can download a replay by visiting a player's profile, clicking on a match, and pressing "Download replay".

> You can see all of your downloaded replays by clicking on the "Replays" button on main Draftout screen.

Replays are stored on the server for 3 days. If you wish to keep the replay, download it within that window. Downloaded replays are stored on your computer until you delete them.

An expired, unavailable or private replay cannot be downloaded.

> Lobby hosts can choose to private the replay, making them only available to the Lobby players.

## Watch a match

Press **B** to open the replay board and controls. Use the timeline to seek through the match. Goal markers on the timeline are clickable, and the bar shows the selected player's dimension.

| Default key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left arrow | Go back 5 seconds |
| Right arrow | Go forward 5 seconds |
| Down arrow | Play or pause replay |
| Up arrow | Switch viewed player |
| K | Teleport to the selected player |
| J | Start or stop following the selected player |

Change the default Replay hotkeys in Minecraft's **Controls** settings under Draftout.

While following a player, you can hold *sneak* or *jump* to adjust the camera distance.

## Keep and share replays

The **Replays** screen lets you rename or delete downloaded matches. You can open the `replays` folder by clicking on the folder icon button.

You can share replay files by simply copying the `.lreplay` files. To view a shared replay, place it in the `replays` folder, and reopen the Replays screen to refresh the list.
