Open **Draftout > Settings** to view all Draftout settings.

## Board

- **Board position** - places the board on the left or right side.
- **Cycle speed** - changes how quickly icons cycle.
- **Board scale** - adjusts the board from 50% to 200% of default size.
- **Completed goals** - lets you configure how completed goals are displayed (color behind/in front of the icon, or using ✔ and ✘ symbols)

## Controls

Open Minecraft's **Controls** settings and find Draftout keybinds.

**Open Board** is bound to **B** by default.

**Toggle Board** is not bound by default, it toggles the visibility of HUD board. The board becomes visible again at the start of each match.

[Replay controls](/wiki/gameplay/replays#watch-a-match) have their own keybinds in the same section.

## Account

Choose your primary and secondary board color. If both opponents choose the same primary color, one player uses their secondary color.

The **Account** tab also lets you link Discord and Twitch.

Linking Discord lets your Supporter subscription appear on Discord (and it gives you access to a #supporter-chat channel).

Linking Twitch connects your Minecraft session to the [Draftout Twitch extension](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/4k57y3zkte8zfbegqvm5ngcgw6whpa-0.0.2). If Twitch account is linked, information about the board's position on your screen (as a % of the Minecraft window) is sent to the server.

## Utility

**Clear old worlds** is enabled by default. **Worlds to keep** sets how many recent Draftout worlds remain, with a default of 5.

Turn this off or increase the limit if you want to keep more worlds for later exploration. This is not recommended, as Draftout worlds tend to be large (up to several GB per world if you explore a lot).

**Disable 'Preset' Slider** is disabled by default. If you enable it, "Preset" slider in Video Settings will be disabled. This slider is usually accidentally clicked in matches, and it changes most of your video settings (render distance, simulation distance).
