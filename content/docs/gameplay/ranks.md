Competitive matches use a Glicko-2 rating system. Your rating changes after each game, based on the outcome (W/D/L), ratings of both players and your Glicko-2 rating volatility.

Rating is only affected by Competitive matches.

## Get ranked

Play several Competitive matches to get placed and receive your rating. This usually takes 4-5 games.

Rating changes are larger early on while the Glicko-2 system "learns" your skill level, and they are lower as you play more matches.

Game score (e.g. winning 13-12 vs 13-5) does NOT affect the rating change.

## Rank tiers

| Rating | Rank | Divisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 499 | Silverfish | I to V |
| 500 to 999 | Slime | I to V |
| 1000 to 1299 | Vex | I to III |
| 1300 to 1599 | Evoker | I to III |
| 1600 to 1799 | Phantom | I to II |
| 1800 to 1999 | Guardian | I to II |
| 2000+ | Warden | - |

Each rank division is exactly 100 rating (e.g. Vex I starts at 1000, Vex II at 1100, and Vex III at 1200).

## Matchmaking

The queue starts by looking for opponents within a rating and rank window.
That window starts at 10 ranks and 50 rating, expanding to 200 ranks and 300 rating over ~30 minutes.

## Leaderboards

Glicko-2 was introduced with the v1.16.0 update and rating reset. View current standings on the [leaderboard](https://draftoutmc.com/leaderboard).
