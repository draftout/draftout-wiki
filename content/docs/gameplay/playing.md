Draftout is a race to complete goals on a shared board. Each player plays in a **separate** local world with the same seed.

Your opponent can't interact with your world. You only share the chat, advancements and the current dimension (on tab).

The first player to complete a goal claims it. That goal is then locked for the opponent. First player to claim the majority of the board wins.

## Choose a mode

| Mode | Board | Time limit | Ranked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Competitive | Drafted 5 × 5 board | 1 hour and 15 minutes, with up to 15 minutes of overtime in case of a tie | Yes |
| Quick Play | Drafted 4 × 4 board with easier, faster goals | 30 minutes | No |
| Lobbies | Random, drafted, or custom board | Chosen by the host | No |

Choose Quick Play or Competitive from the Draftout menu to join the queue and play against a random opponent. Use [Lobbies](/wiki/gameplay/lobbies) to play with friends or practice alone.

Both Quick Play and Competitive begin with a [draft](/wiki/gameplay/drafting) where you and your opponent choose the goals for the match.

## Board and goals

Press **B** to open the board and read goal names. Goals complete automatically when you meet their requirements. Some goals also show progress in chat, as well as when you hover them.

Use the [goal index](/wiki/goals) for completion requirements and guides.

Some goals require having several items at once in your inventory, keep those items until the goal completes.

Goal claims account for connection latency. If both players finish the same goal close together, ping adjustment can change who receives it.

## How to win?

You win when your opponent can no longer catch up. As long as your opponent can at least get a draw, the game does not end.

- In Competitive mode (25 goals), 13 goals secure the win.
- In Quick Play mode (16 goals), 9 goals secure the win (8-8 ties are possible).

If the time limit expires, the player with the most completed goals wins. 

Tied Competitive matches have an overtime where the next player to complete any remaining goal wins. If neither player completes one within 15 minutes, the match is a draw. Tied Quick Play match ends in a draw without overtime.

The board screen also has **Draw vote** and **Forfeit match** buttons.

After a Quick Play match, you can use **Rematch** to request another game with the same opponent.

## After the match

Open your profile to review your match history. Competitive matches can also be reviewed with [replays](/wiki/gameplay/replays).

Use **Spectate World** on the post-match screen to explore the world. Commands, including `/seed`, are available there.
