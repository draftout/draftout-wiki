Draftout is a race to claim goals before your opponent. Players use the same seed in separate worlds, and each completed goal is locked for the other player.

## Start playing

- [Getting started](/wiki/gameplay/getting-started): Install Draftout and join your first match.
- [Playing a match](/wiki/gameplay/playing): Choose a mode, use the board, and understand how matches end.
- [Drafting](/wiki/gameplay/drafting): Scout the world, choose goals, and use your reroll.
- [Ranks and rating](/wiki/gameplay/ranks): Get ranked and understand rating changes.

## Learn and practise

- [Seeds](/wiki/gameplay/seeds): Learn the seed types and reuse a world for practice.
- [Changes to Vanilla](/wiki/gameplay/standardization): Understand shared drops, barters, and container loot.
- [Lobbies](/wiki/gameplay/lobbies): Play with friends, practise alone, and create custom boards.
- [Replays](/wiki/gameplay/replays): Download Competitive matches and review either player's route.

## Configure the game

- [Settings](/wiki/gameplay/settings): Adjust the board, controls, and other options.
- [Troubleshooting](/wiki/gameplay/troubleshooting): Resolve launch, connection, and display problems.

For a specific goal, browse the [goal index](/wiki/goals). Read the [Draftout rules](https://draftoutmc.com/rules) before joining a queue.
