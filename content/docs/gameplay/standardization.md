Draftout standardizes many random outcomes between players.
This means the game uses the exact same random sequences for actions such as Piglin bartering, taming and mob drops, so players on the same seed get roughly the same outcome.

## Shared drops

These loot tables are standardized:
- Leather from cows, horses, donkeys, and mules are grouped into one sequence (order of killing said mobs doesn't matter; killing the same number of these mobs will give the same amount of leather).
- Each ore (stone and deepslate variant) share one sequence (e.g. Lapis Ore and Deepslate Lapis ore).
- Grass and fern blocks are grouped together.
- Mob equipment drops are standardized (Pillager crossbows, Witches' held potions, Piglin armor etc.).

Pillager captain spawns are standardized by spawn number. This does not guarantee a captain at a fixed time or position.

## Piglin bartering

First 144 Gold Ingots (16 Gold Blocks) bartered with Piglins are guaranteed to produce at least 4 Ender Pearl trades (2-4 pearls each). Additional Ender Pearl trades are possible, but they are not guaranteed (it is determined by the world seed).

## Other standardizations

### Nether portals

If your first Nether exit creates a new Overworld portal, standing at Y 55 or higher (in the Nether) will create an Overworld portal on the surface.

### Food effects

Food effects are also standardized.
- Hunger from Rotten Flesh
- Hunger from Raw Chicken
- Poison from Poisonous Potato

### Iron Golem damage

In Vanilla, the damage that Iron Golem deals is random, and it varies significantly (7.5 base damage + up to 15 *random* additional damage, before accounting for difficulty).

In Draftout, this damage is standardized, but only for player damage.

### Chest Loot

Chest loot follows vanilla generation.

> An update briefly changed containers to use opening order when generating loot, but that change was reverted.
> Players can still access this feature using the 'standardized_container_loot_order' gamerule.
