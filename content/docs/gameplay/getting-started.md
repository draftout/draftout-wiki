## Install Draftout

Follow the [installation guide](https://draftoutmc.com/download) for up-to-date download and launcher instructions.

Draftout currently requires:
- Minecraft 26.3,
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer,
- Java 25 or newer,
- At least 3 GB of memory allocated to Minecraft

The installation guide also links to allowed mods, mostly for performance and accessibility. 

Draftout will prompt you in-game when an update is available.

> Draftout Modpack will soon be available which will make updating to newer Minecraft versions easier. Until then, as Draftout updates to newer Minecraft versions, players must make a new instance to continue playing Draftout.

## Play your first match

Launch the instance and open **Draftout**.

Choose **Quick Play** for a 4x4 board, or create a [Lobby](/wiki/gameplay/lobbies) to play alone or with friends. For solo practice, set teams to **Single**.
In Quick Play, you [draft the board](/wiki/gameplay/drafting) with your opponent.

After the countdown ends, you can start completing goals. Press **B** to view the board.

See [Playing a match](/wiki/gameplay/playing) for scoring, time limits and Competitive matches.

## Before you play Competitive

Make sure to read the [Draftout rules](https://draftoutmc.com/rules)!
Some tools used in Minecraft speedrunning are banned in Draftout (notably Stronghold calculators).

You are allowed to manually look up an individual goal in this wiki (or Minecraft wiki) during a match.

In Competitive matches, coaching and backseating are not allowed. Players must make their own decisions.

If the game does not launch, view [Troubleshooting](/wiki/gameplay/troubleshooting) or join our Discord.
