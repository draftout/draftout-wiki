All players in a match play on the same world seed, but in their own separate worlds (players can NOT interact, other than using the in-game chat).

Quick Play and Competitive modes choose a seed from Draftout's filtered pool. The seed type is hidden, so use the [scouting phase](/wiki/gameplay/drafting#scout-the-world) to scout the board and your line in the world.

## Seed types

The seed pool contains 11 seed types:

| Seed Type | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Village | 100 (16.3%) |
| Abandoned Camp | 100 (16.3%) |
| Shipwreck | 100 (16.3%) |
| Pillager Outpost | 100 (16.3%) |
| Desert Temple | 50 (8.2%) |
| Jungle Temple | 50 (8.2%) |
| Witch Hut | 40 (6.5%) |
| Mansion | 30 (4.9%) |
| Igloo | 20 (3.2%) |
| Ancient City | 10 (1.6%) |
| Pale Garden | 10 (1.6%) |

The intended structure/biome is just an option, you don't have to use it.

## Filtered worlds

Draftout's filtered Nether seeds include a [Bastion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bastion_Remnant) and a [Nether Fortress](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress) within 250 blocks of (0, 0). Nether Wart is always available (either a Housing Bastion or inside the Fortress room).

Draftout also attempts to place 3 lava pools 50 to 200 away blocks from spawn. Rarely, terrain can prevent the spawn attempt, so this is not a guarantee of three Lava pools (though at least 2 should usually spawn).

Other surface Lava pools spawns are also boosted (2x the vanilla rates). Read more in [Changes to Vanilla](/wiki/gameplay/standardization).

## Practice a seed

In [Lobby settings](/wiki/gameplay/lobbies), enter a **World Seed** to spawn in a specific world, or leave it empty to use a random seed from the seed pool.

Lobby host can use **Change Seed** before game starts when no world seed is set. Selecting which seed types can appear requires **Supporter Tier 2** or higher.

After a match, **Spectate World** lets you explore the world. During Quick Play and Competitive matches, the [rules](https://draftoutmc.com/rules) prohibit players trying to learn or use the world seed.
