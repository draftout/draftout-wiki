## Construct an Iron Golem

Completes when you earn the [Hired Help](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Hired_Help) advancement by summoning an [Iron Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#Creation).

## Find a Stronghold

Completes when you earn the [Eye Spy](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stronghold#Advancements) advancement by throwing an [Eye of Ender](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Eye_of_Ender#Locating_strongholds) and following it into a [stronghold](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stronghold#Generation).

## Fully charge a Respawn Anchor

Completes when you earn the [Not Quite "Nine" Lives](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Not_Quite_%22Nine%22_Lives) advancement by fully charging a [Respawn Anchor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Respawn_Anchor#Advancements) to all 4 charges.

## Get any Spyglass Advancement

Completes when you earn any of the 3 [spyglass advancements](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass#Advancements) by looking through a [Spyglass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass) at a [Parrot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Parrot), [Ghast](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast), or [Ender Dragon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon).

## Obtain "Bullseye" Advancement

Completes when you earn the [Bullseye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Target#Advancements) advancement by hitting the bullseye of a [Target](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Target) block with a [projectile](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Projectile#Table) from at least 30 blocks away **horizontally**.

## Obtain "Mob Kabob" Advancement

Completes when you earn the [Mob Kabob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Mob_Kabob) advancement by targeting 5 mobs in range with a [Spear](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spear) during one continuous charge, then landing a hit.

## Obtain "Oh Shiny" Advancement

Completes when you earn the [Oh Shiny](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Oh_Shiny) advancement by giving an [aggravated Piglin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#Aggravation) one of the [gold-related items it loves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#Piglin_loved_items).

## Obtain "Sniper Duel" Advancement

Completes when you earn the [Sniper Duel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Sniper_Duel) advancement by getting kill credit for a [Skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton) that is at least 50 blocks away **horizontally** and is killed with a [projectile](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Projectile#Table).

## Scrape wax off of Copper Block

Completes when you earn the [Wax Off](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Wax_Off) advancement by using an [Axe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axe) to scrape the wax off any waxed [copper block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper#Oxidizing_blocks).

## Trade with a Villager

Completes when you earn the [What a Deal!](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#What_a_Deal!) advancement by taking an item from the trade output of a [Villager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager) or [Wandering Trader](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wandering_Trader).

## Wax a Copper Block

Completes when you earn the [Wax On](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Wax_On) advancement by using a [Honeycomb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Honeycomb) on any waxable [copper block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper#Oxidizing_blocks). 

## Find a Fortress

Completes when you earn the [A Terrible Fortress](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#A_Terrible_Fortress) advancement by entering a [Nether Fortress](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress).

## Obtain "The Power of Books" Advancement

Completes when you earn [The Power of Books](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#The_Power_of_Books) advancement by reading a [Chiseled Bookshelf](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chiseled_Bookshelf#Redstone_signal) with a [Redstone Comparator](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Comparator).

## Obtain "Stay Hydrated!" Advancement

Completes when you earn the [Stay Hydrated!](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Stay_Hydrated!) advancement by waterlogging a [Dried Ghast](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dried_Ghast#Usage).

## Find an End City

Completes when you earn [The City at the End of the Game](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#The_City_at_the_End_of_the_Game) advancement by entering an [End City](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_City).

## Find a Bastion

Completes when you earn the [Those Were the Days](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Those_Were_the_Days) advancement by entering a [Bastion Remnant](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bastion_Remnant).
