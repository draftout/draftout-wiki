title: "Status Effects at Once"

## Get 3 Status Effects at once

Completes when you gain a [status effect](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Effect#List_of_effects) while you have at least 3 active status effects, including the effect you just gained.

## Get 4 Status Effects at once

Completes when you gain a [status effect](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Effect#List_of_effects) while you have at least 4 active status effects, including the effect you just gained.

## Get 6 Status Effects at once

Completes when you gain a [status effect](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Effect#List_of_effects) while you have at least 6 active status effects, including the effect you just gained.
