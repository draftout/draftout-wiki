## Put Wolf Armor on Wolf

Completes when you put [Wolf Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#Wolf_armor) on a [Wolf](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf).

## Wear a Carved Pumpkin for 5 minutes

Completes when you have worn a [Carved Pumpkin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carved_Pumpkin#Helmet) on your head for a cumulative 5 minutes.

## Wear a Chain Armor Piece

Completes when you wear any [Chainmail Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chainmail_Armor#List_of_items) piece.

## Wear Full Enchanted Armor

Completes when you have [enchanted](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanting) items in all 4 [armor slots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor#List_of_armor_items) at once.

## Wear Full Leather Armor, in different colors

Completes when you wear all 4 [Leather Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leather_Armor#Dyeing_leather_armor) pieces at once, each dyed a different non-default color.

## Wear Full Armor with different materials

Completes when you wear a Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots made from 4 different [armor materials](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor#Armor_materials).
