## Kill 20 Arthropods

Completes when you get kill credit for 20 [arthropod](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arthropod#List_of_mobs) mobs, counting any of these mob types: [Bee](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee), [Cave Spider](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cave_Spider), [Spider](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider), [Endermite](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Endermite), [Silverfish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silverfish).

## Kill 30 Undead Mobs

Completes when you get kill credit for 30 [undead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Undead#List_of_mobs) mobs, counting any of these mob types: [Bogged](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bogged), [Camel Husk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Camel_Husk), [Drowned](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned), [Husk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Husk), [Parched](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Parched), [Phantom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom), [Skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton), [Skeleton Horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_Horse), [Stray](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stray), [Wither](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither), [Wither Skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_Skeleton), [Zoglin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zoglin), [Zombie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie), [Zombie Horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Horse), [Zombie Nautilus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Nautilus), [Zombie Villager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Villager), [Zombified Piglin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin).

## Kill all Raid Mobs

Completes when you get kill credit for every [Raid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid) [mob type](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager#List_of_mobs): [Pillager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager), [Vindicator](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vindicator), [Ravager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager), [Witch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Witch), [Vex](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vex), and [Evoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker).
