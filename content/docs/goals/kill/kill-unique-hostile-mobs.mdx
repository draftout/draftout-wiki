## Kill 10 Unique Hostile Mobs

Completes when you get kill credit for 10 different [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monster#List_of_monsters) types.

[Skeleton Horses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_Horse), [Zombie Horses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Horse), [Zombie Nautiluses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Nautilus), and [Camel Husks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Camel_Husk) also count if they have a hostile passenger.
