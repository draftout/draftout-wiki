## Die by Drowning

Completes when you die from [drowning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Drowning).

## Die by falling in Void

Completes when you die from falling out of the world into [the void](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Void#Java_Edition).

## Die by falling off Vines

Completes when you die from fall damage after climbing [vines](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vines), [twisting vines](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Twisting_Vines), [weeping vines](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weeping_Vines), or [cave vines](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_Berries#Climbing).

## Die by Freezing

Completes when you die to [freezing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Powder_Snow#Freezing) damage.

## Die to [Intentional Game Design]

Completes when you die from a [Bed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bed#Explosions) or [Respawn Anchor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Respawn_Anchor#Exploding) explosion caused by using it in the wrong dimension.

## Die to Berry Bush

Completes when you die to a [Sweet Berry Bush](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_Berries#Entity_movement).

## Die to Cactus

Completes when you die to a [Cactus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cactus#Usage).

## Die to falling Anvil

Completes when you die to a falling [Anvil](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Anvil#Falling_anvils).

## Die to Falling Stalactite

## Die to Firework Rocket
