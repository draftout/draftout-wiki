## Die by Magic

Completes when you die to [magic damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Magic_damage), including indirect magic such as a thrown potion.

## Die to Bee Sting

Completes when you die to a [Bee](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee#Attacking) sting.

## Die to Iron Golem

Completes when you die with an [Iron Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#Attacking) as the attacking entity.

## Die to Polar Bear

Completes when you die with a [Polar Bear](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_Bear#Behavior) as the attacking entity.

## Die to Pufferfish

Completes when you die with a [Pufferfish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pufferfish#Defenses) as the attacking entity.

## Die to Trident

Completes when you die to [Trident](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trident#Drowned) damage.

## Die to Warden

Completes when you die with a [Warden](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#Attacks) as the attacking entity.
