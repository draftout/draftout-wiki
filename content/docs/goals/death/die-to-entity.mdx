## Die by Magic

Completes when you die to [magic damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Magic_damage), including indirect magic such as a thrown potion.

Note that when using Guardians for this goal, **only a portion** of their attack damage uses the magic damage type. When set to Easy or Normal difficulty, this is 1 point of damage (half a heart), and on Hard, this is 4.5 points (less than 2 hearts and a half). Because the magic damage is ticked first, this means **you have to be at 1 health to get this goal from a Guardian on Easy or Normal, and at 4.5 health on Hard.**

## Die to Bee Sting

Completes when you die to a [Bee](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee#Attacking) sting.

## Die to Iron Golem

Completes when you die with an [Iron Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#Attacking) as the attacking entity.

## Die to Sulfur Cube

Completes when you die with a [Sulfur Cube](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sulfur_Cube) as the attacking entity, either by explosion from TNT inside Sulfur Cube or damage from Magma inside Sulfur Cube.
