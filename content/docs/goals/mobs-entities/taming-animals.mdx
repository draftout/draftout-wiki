## Tame a Cat

Completes when you tame a [Cat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cat#Taming).

## Tame a Horse

Completes when you tame a [Horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Horse#Taming) or [Zombie Horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Horse#Taming).

## Tame a Nautilus

Completes when you tame a [Nautilus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nautilus#Taming).

## Tame a Parrot

Completes when you tame a [Parrot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Parrot#Behavior).
