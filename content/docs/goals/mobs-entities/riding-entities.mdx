## Ride a Minecart

Completes when you are riding a standard [Minecart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecart).

## Ride Pig with Carrot on a Stick

Completes when you use a [Carrot on a Stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carrot_on_a_Stick#Usage) to boost a [Pig](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pig#Riding) while riding it.

## Ride a Horse

Completes when you ride a [Horse](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Horse#Taming). The Horse does not need to be tamed or saddled.
