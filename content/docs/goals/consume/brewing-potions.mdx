## Brew a Lingering Potion

Completes when you take any [Lingering Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lingering_Potion) from a [Brewing Stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand) potion slot.

## Brew a Potion of Healing

Completes when you take a [Potion of Healing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion_of_Healing) from a [Brewing Stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand#Brewing) potion slot. The strong variant also counts.
