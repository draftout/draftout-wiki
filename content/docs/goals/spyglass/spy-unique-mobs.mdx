## Spy on 10 Unique Mobs

Completes when you have looked at 10 different [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob#List_of_mobs) types through a [Spyglass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass#Usage).

## Spy on 15 Unique Mobs

Completes when you have looked at 15 different [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob#List_of_mobs) types through a [Spyglass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass#Usage).

## Spy on 20 Unique Mobs

Completes when you have looked at 20 different [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob#List_of_mobs) types through a [Spyglass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass#Usage).

## Spy on 25 Unique Mobs

Completes when you have looked at 25 different [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob#List_of_mobs) types through a [Spyglass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spyglass#Usage).
