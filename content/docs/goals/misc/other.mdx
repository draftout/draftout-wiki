## Break any Armor Piece

Completes when you break an [armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor#Durability) piece from durability damage. Leather, copper, golden, chainmail, iron, and diamond armor pieces count.

## Explode End Crystal

Completes when you cause an [End Crystal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_Crystal#Explosions) to explode with damage credited to you.

## Ride a Geyser

Completes when you are launched by a [geyser](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potent_Sulfur#Geysers).

## Sit on Colored Cushion

Completes when you sit on a [Cushion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cushion#Usage) of the goal variant's color.
