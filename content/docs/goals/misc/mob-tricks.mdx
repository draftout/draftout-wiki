## Enrage Enderman

Completes when you become an [Enderman](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#Provoking)'s target.

## Enrage Zombified Piglin

Completes when you become a [Zombified Piglin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin#Hostility)'s anger target.

## Name a Ghast 'Dinnerbone'

Completes when you use a [Name Tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_Tag#Easter_eggs) named `Dinnerbone` or `Grumm` on a [Ghast](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast).

## Name an Iron Golem 'Dinnerbone'

Completes when you use a [Name Tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_Tag#Easter_eggs) named `Dinnerbone` or `Grumm` on an [Iron Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem).

## Name a Sheep 'jeb_'

Completes when you use a [Name Tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_Tag#Easter_eggs) to name a Sheep `jeb_`.

## Duplicate Allay

Completes when you duplicate an [Allay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#Allay_duplication) by giving it an Amethyst Shard while it is dancing.
