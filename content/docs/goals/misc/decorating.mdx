## Decorate Shield with Banner

Completes when you have a [Shield](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shield#Applying_patterns) with a banner applied in your inventory.

## Light a Candle

Completes when you use [Flint and Steel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flint_and_Steel) or a [Fire Charge](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fire_Charge) on a lightable [Candle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Candle#Light).

## Use Glow Ink on Crimson or Warped Sign

Completes when you successfully apply a [Glow Ink Sac](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_Ink_Sac) to a [Crimson Sign](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sign#Text), [Crimson Hanging Sign](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hanging_Sign#Text), [Warped Sign](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sign#Text) or [Warped Hanging Sign](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hanging_Sign#Text).
