## Visit All Nether Biomes

Completes when you visit all 5 [Nether biomes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether#Biomes): [Nether Wastes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wastes), [Crimson Forest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crimson_Forest), [Warped Forest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_Forest), [Soul Sand Valley](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_Sand_Valley), and [Basalt Deltas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Basalt_Deltas).

## Teleport through Nether Portal using Ender Pearl

Completes when your thrown [Ender Pearl](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Pearl#Collisions) lands and teleports you between the Overworld and the Nether.

## Visit all Cave Biomes

Completes when you visit [Lush Caves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lush_Caves), [Dripstone Caves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dripstone_Caves), and the [Deep Dark](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_Dark).
