---
title: "Reaching Heights"
---

## Get on Nether Roof

Completes when your [Y coordinate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Altitude) is 128 or higher while you are in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether).

## Reach Bedrock

Completes when you're above [Bedrock](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock) at the bottom of the world.

Reaching Nether roof does not count.

## Reach height limit in Overworld

Completes when your [Y coordinate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Altitude) is at least 320 in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld), or at least 256 in other dimensions.
