## Obtain 3 types of Horse Armor

Completes when you have 3 different [Horse Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Horse_Armor#Variants) items in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 3 Unique Banner Patterns

Completes when you have 3 different [Banner Pattern](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Banner_Pattern#Variants) items in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 4 Unique Mushrooms

Completes when you have 4 of these in your inventory at once: [Red Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mushroom), [Brown Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mushroom), [Crimson Fungus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fungus), [Warped Fungus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fungus), and [Shelf Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shelf_Mushroom).

## Obtain 4 Unique Saplings

Completes when you have 4 different [saplings](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sapling#Growing_trees) in your inventory at once. [Azalea](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Azalea), [Flowering Azalea](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Azalea), and [Mangrove Propagule](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mangrove_Propagule) also count.

## Obtain 4 Unique Seeds

Completes when you have 4 different seed items in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 6 Unique Buckets

Completes when you have 6 different [Bucket](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bucket#Usage) items in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 6 Unique Flowers

Completes when you have 6 different items from the goal's [flower](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flower#Variants) list in your inventory at once.
