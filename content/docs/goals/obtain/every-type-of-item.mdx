---
title: "Every type of Item"
---

## Obtain every type of Furnace

Completes when you have a [Furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Furnace), [Blast Furnace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blast_Furnace), and [Smoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smoker) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain every type of Mushroom

Completes when you have a [Red Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mushroom), [Brown Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mushroom), [Crimson Fungus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fungus), and [Warped Fungus](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fungus) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain every type of Pumpkin

Completes when you have a [Pumpkin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pumpkin), [Carved Pumpkin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carved_Pumpkin), and [Jack o'Lantern](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jack_o%27Lantern) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain every type of Raw Ore Block

Completes when you have a [Block of Raw Copper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Raw_Copper), [Block of Raw Gold](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Raw_Gold), and [Block of Raw Iron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block_of_Raw_Iron) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain every type of Torch

Completes when you have a [Torch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Torch), [Redstone Torch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Torch), [Soul Torch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_Torch), and [Copper Torch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper_Torch) in your inventory at once.
