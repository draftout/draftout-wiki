## Obtain 64 Arrows

Completes when you have at least 64 [Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arrow) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 64 Colored Concrete

Completes when you have at least 64 [Concrete](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Concrete) blocks of the goal variant's color in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 64 Colored Wool

Completes when you have at least 64 [Wool](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wool) blocks of the goal variant's color in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 64 Coarse Dirt

Completes when you have at least 64 [Coarse Dirt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coarse_Dirt) in your inventory at once.

## Obtain 32 Arrows

Completes when you have at least 32 [Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arrow) or at least 32 [Spectral Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectral_Arrow) in your main inventory at once.
They are not additive, you must have at least 32 of either type.

## Obtain a stack of any Item

Completes when you have a single stack of 64 items in your main [inventory](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Inventory#Managing_inventory).
