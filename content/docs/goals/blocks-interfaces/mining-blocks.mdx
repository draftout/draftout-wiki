## Mine Crafter

Completes when you break a [Crafter](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafter).

## Mine Diamond Ore

Completes when you break [Diamond Ore](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Diamond_Ore) or [Deepslate Diamond Ore](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Diamond_Ore#Natural_generation).

## Mine Emerald Ore

Completes when you break [Emerald Ore](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Emerald_Ore) or [Deepslate Emerald Ore](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Emerald_Ore#Natural_generation).

## Mine Mob Spawner
