## Fill a Decorated Pot

Completes when you use an item on a [Decorated Pot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Decorated_Pot) and the pot's stored item stack reaches its maximum stack size.

## Fill an Armor Stand

Completes when you successfully interact with an [Armor Stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor_Stand#Equipping_armor) and leave items in its head, chest, legs, and feet slots.

## Fill Campfire with 4 Food Items

Completes when you place food on a [Campfire](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire#Cooking) and fill its last empty cooking slot.

## Fill Chiseled Bookshelf

Completes when you place a book in a [Chiseled Bookshelf](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chiseled_Bookshelf) and fill its last empty slot.

## Fill Composter and get Bone Meal

Completes when you empty a full [Composter](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Composter#Composting) and collect its [Bone Meal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_Meal).

<Resources ids={["early-game-practice-map"]} />

## Fill Inventory with unique items

Completes when you fill all 36 main [inventory](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Inventory) slots, including the hotbar, with a different item type in each slot.
