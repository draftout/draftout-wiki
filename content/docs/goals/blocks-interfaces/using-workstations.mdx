## Use Anvil

Completes when you take the output item from an [Anvil](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Anvil#Usage).

## Use Brewing Stand

Completes when you earn the [Local Brewery](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement#Local_Brewery) advancement by taking a [Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion) from a [Brewing Stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand#Brewing) potion slot.

## Use Cauldron to wash something

Completes when you use a [Cauldron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cauldron) to clean dyed leather armor, a [Banner](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Banner), or a [Shulker Box](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker_Box).
