## Break any Tool

Completes when you break a tool from durability damage. 

Wooden, stone, copper, golden, iron, and diamond axes, hoes, pickaxes, swords, shovels, spears, [Fishing Rods](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fishing_Rod), [Flint and Steel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flint_and_Steel), [Shears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shears), [Brushes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brush), [Carrots on a Stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carrot_on_a_Stick), and [Warped Fungus on a Stick](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_Fungus_on_a_Stick) count.

## Shoot Firework from Crossbow

Completes when you fire a [Crossbow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crossbow) loaded with a [Firework Rocket](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Firework_Rocket).

## Use Brush on Suspicious Sand/Gravel

Completes when you finish brushing a [Suspicious Sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Suspicious_Sand) or [Suspicious Gravel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Suspicious_Gravel) block.
